About this demo. The Viber, ServiceTitan, Procore, PlanSwift and Textura screens above are recreations of the real products, built for this walkthrough; the companies, people and numbers are fictional. What is real is the shape of the work: the systems touched, the order, the checks, and the gate. People appear only in the messenger; the systems only ever show Nova working alone, and each system scene says at the top why Nova is there. Each step under the window is tagged the way the recipe treats it: fixed where the route is known, judgment kept where it is needed, and a person on every write that leaves the building.

That was one recipe. Your week has a dozen leaks like it.

You do not have to know which process to fix. You have to notice what annoyed you this week and say it out loud. Discovery runs at our cost; nothing touches your systems until you say go.
