01

The problem

Every team runs on work nobody ever wrote down. It lives in the heads of the people who always handle it.

The check that follows the same policy. The reconciliation that traces the same four systems. The exception that follows the same five rules.

It repeats dozens of times a week, it costs your most senior people their afternoons, and when they leave it walks out with them.

02

What Nova does

Most of a working day is transport. A task arrives in Slack or a shared inbox. Someone opens one system to get the facts, opens a second to check them against the rule, makes a call that follows the same logic it followed last time, and types the outcome into a third.

The systems do not talk to each other, so a person is the thing carrying the context between them. Nova connects to those same systems and does the carrying.

04

Why we built it this way

Most AI agents sell more autonomy. Nova is the opposite bet: an Agent that does less on its own over time, because what it learns becomes a fixed, inspectable, rigid flow.

Self-improvement that increases predictability, not autonomy. That is the whole product. The market's bet is more autonomy over time — the agent decides more on its own, and you trust more and see less. Nova's bet is more predictability: each repeat hardens into a rigid, versioned recipe you can read.

A human still approves every change that touches a customer, money, or production — and you never configure Nova or pick which workflow to automate. Your team just uses it, and it codifies what they actually do.

05

Your data, your models, your controls

Nova is built for teams that can't be casual about data, access, or spend — in plain terms:

Privacy

Your data stays yours — and never trains anyone's model

Nova runs in a dedicated, fully isolated environment. Your data is never pooled with other customers, never shared with the AI provider, and never used to train anyone's models.

Models

Model-agnostic — you choose the brain

Run Nova on OpenAI, Anthropic, or open-source models you host yourself, and keep the most sensitive work on a model that never leaves your environment. Switch providers without losing a day of accumulated context.

Access

Only what the person tasking it could do

Nova works with that person's access — no more — and a human approves anything that touches a customer, money, or production. All access runs through a standalone broker: least privilege, deny by default, every call logged and reviewable.

Spend

Costs you can see and cap

Every AI call is metered, so you see exactly what Nova spends and can set limits per team — no surprise bills.

Controls

The enterprise basics, covered

Single sign-on, role-based access, and a full audit trail. Data residency agreed per deployment.
