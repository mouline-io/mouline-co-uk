Get started with Nova

Nova is an AI agent that works in your Slack workspace. You set her up yourself, from Slack and a browser, and the first week is free.

01Before you start

  • A Slack workspace where you can install apps. If you aren't a workspace admin, ask one to do step 2.
  • An Anthropic API key. Create one at console.anthropic.com. Nova runs on it, and Anthropic bills you for that usage directly.
  • No card. You only need one when you choose a plan.

02Add Nova to Slack

Open control.mouline.co.uk/install, click Add to Slack and approve the permissions Slack shows you. Whoever completes the install becomes the workspace's first Nova admin. Enterprise Grid org-wide installs aren't supported; install into one workspace.

03Add your Anthropic key

Sign in with Slack at control.mouline.co.uk. This is Nova's dashboard, and it shows only your workspace. Open Settings, paste your key under Anthropic API key and save.

Until a key is saved, Nova answers every mention with a link to Settings and does no work.

04Bring Nova into a channel

In the Slack channel where you want Nova to work:

  1. Invite her: /invite @Nova
  2. Enable her there: /nova channel add (Nova admins only)

By default Nova answers when someone mentions her. To have her pick up every new message in a channel, such as a support or requests channel, run /nova channel auto on there. /nova channel auto off switches back.

05Add your team

Nova only answers people you've allowed. Add someone with /nova user add @name in Slack, or on the dashboard's Access page. During the trial up to 10 people can use Nova, admins included. On Business, there's no limit.

06Give her real work

Mention @Nova in a thread and ask for a task your team does every week. Each Slack thread is one ongoing conversation, so reply in the same thread to follow up or correct her. A person reviews anything consequential before it happens.

The dashboard shows what Nova did in each conversation and what it cost, and lets admins manage access, integrations, egress rules, scheduled jobs and settings.

07Connect Google Drive

Optional, and worth a few trial days. With Drive connected, Nova finds, reads and edits your Docs, Sheets and Slides, creates and copies files, organises folders, and shares files with people in your company. In Sheets she changes only the cells she means to, appends rows, adds tabs and formats them, without rewriting the rest. You decide, channel by channel, which shared drives she may use.

Turn on the Shadow Broker

Nova reaches Drive through a Shadow Broker, which holds the Google credentials so Nova never sees them. In Settings, under Shadow Broker, choose Run by Mouline and save. Mouline then runs a broker for your workspace alone. There's nothing to install and no key to copy.

Connect your Google account

Open Integrations and click Connect Google Drive. Google asks you to sign in and approve access; Nova then acts as that Google account, so she sees what it sees. You come back to the Google Drive page, where Nova can use all of Drive in every channel until you narrow it.

If you'd rather hand over a few folders only, open the Google Drive page before connecting and click Pick folders instead. Nova then reaches just the folders you pick in Google's picker and the files she creates, everywhere. Channel rules need the whole-Drive connection.

Choose where Nova can use Drive

Drive access is set by rules, each written as a sentence on the Google Drive page:

  • Everywhere applies to every channel without a rule of its own: All of Drive, Only some shared drives, or Nowhere.
  • A channel rule covers one or more channels. Click + Add a rule for specific channels, add the channels, and choose all of Drive or some shared drives. You can add a note for Nova too, such as where budgets live.

The most specific rule wins: a channel's own rule replaces Everywhere there, so a channel can get less than Everywhere or more. If two rules name the same channel, Nova gets everything either one allows, and the page marks a rule that adds nothing as Changes nothing. Before you save, the rule shows what Nova will reach after saving.

For example, with Everywhere set to Only Company and a #finance rule for Finance and Company, Nova in #finance can use both drives and nothing else.

See who can reach what

The Integrations page lists every channel with what Nova can reach there. Grey means the channel gets it from Everywhere; black means its own rule decides. Click a channel to see why, or click its Drive cell to change its rule.

Check it's working

Drive has one status on both pages. Working means Nova can use it. Needs attention and Not working come with a banner saying what's wrong and, where the dashboard can fix it, a button. The Health list on the Google Drive page shows each check:

CheckIf it fails
Nova has loaded the connectionNova picks changes up within a minute. If it stays, write to us. Meanwhile Nova tells people Drive is temporarily unavailable.
Network access to GoogleYour egress policy doesn't allow all four Google hosts Drive needs. Click Allow Google hosts, or remove the deny rule under Open network policy.
Used in at least one channelNo rule gives Nova Drive anywhere. Click Add a rule, or set Everywhere.

Sharing, reconnecting and disconnecting

  • Nova shares files only with people in your own Google Workspace domain, and refuses anyone outside it.
  • Reconnect on the Google Drive page signs in to Google again, for example to use another account.
  • Disconnect stops Nova using Drive in every channel at once. Your rules are kept. If Everywhere was set to Nowhere, it comes back as All of Drive when you reconnect, so set it again afterwards.

08Your free trial

Every new workspace gets a 7-day free trial. It starts the first time Nova answers someone, not when you install, so setup doesn't use it up.

  • Up to 3 channels and 10 people during the trial. Going over is refused with a message naming the limit; removing one frees a slot.
  • The dashboard shows the trial's end date, and Nova sends admins a reminder in Slack. Nobody else gets them.
  • Removing and reinstalling Nova keeps the trial where it was.

Earn more days

Trying more of Nova extends the trial, by up to 30 extra working days (Monday to Friday) in total. Each counts once:

Do thisEarn
Save a Shadow Broker connection in Settings, such as Run by Mouline+3 working days
Add your first egress allow rule on the dashboard+3 working days
Have your first scheduled job run successfully+3 working days
Connect Google Drive in Integrations+3 working days
Have Nova edit a Google Sheet for the first time+2 working days
Each new person who gets an answer from Nova+2 working days

The first person's answer starts the trial, so it doesn't earn days. Days earned before the trial starts still count.

When the trial ends

If you haven't chosen a plan, Nova pauses for everyone in the workspace. The Slack install and your data are kept for 30 days, and admins can still sign in to the dashboard. Choosing Business brings her back straight away. If you need more time, write to founders@mouline.work.

Prices are in US dollars. Anthropic bills you separately for the usage on your key.

To pay for Business

  1. Sign in to the dashboard and open Settings. Admins also see a Choose Business button on the trial banner.
  2. Click Choose Business. Stripe Checkout opens.
  3. Pick how many channels Nova can be in, agree to the Terms, and pay by card.

You can pay at any time during the trial. The first month is charged at once, the trial ends, and the trial limits lift straight away. Settings then shows your plan and number of channels.

10Changing or cancelling

In Settings, Manage billing opens Stripe's billing portal. There you can:

  • Add channels. Takes effect at once, charged pro rata for the rest of the month.
  • Remove channels. Takes effect at your next renewal.
  • Update your card and download invoices.
  • Cancel. Nova keeps working until the end of the month you paid for.

We don't refund part months; see the refund policy. If a payment fails, Nova pauses and comes back as soon as the payment goes through.

12Questions

What does Nova cost in total?

Your plan, plus what Anthropic charges for the usage on your own key. Mouline doesn't mark it up.

What counts as a channel?

Each Slack channel where Nova is enabled with /nova channel add, including channels set to auto-reply. On Business you pay for the number of channels you pick, however many you've enabled so far.

Who can change settings and billing?

Nova admins. The person who installs is the first; add more on the dashboard's Access page or with /nova user add @name admin.

Where does my data live?

On Business, Mouline hosts Nova for you, and each workspace gets its own database and its own Nova. On Enterprise, Nova runs in your own network. See the Privacy Policy.

Can Nova see all of our Google Drive?

Only what the Google account you connected can see, narrowed by your rules: Everywhere, and any channel's own rule. The Google credentials stay in your Shadow Broker, and Nova never holds them. She shares files only inside your company's Google domain.

How do I remove Nova?

Cancel under Manage billing if you pay, then remove the app from Slack. After you cancel, we keep your data for 30 days so you can come back, then delete it.

Anything else: founders@mouline.work.
