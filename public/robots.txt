# Prospect pages under /x/ are deliberately NOT disallowed here. They already
# carry <meta name="robots" content="noindex,nofollow,noarchive">, and a
# Disallow rule would stop crawlers fetching the page at all, so they would
# never read that noindex. It would also advertise the path to anyone reading
# this file. Crawl-and-obey-noindex keeps them out of indexes more reliably
# than blocking the crawl does.
